## Vision

### 10x Check
The owner opens ONE thing in the morning. Not GHL, not Stripe, not Sheets — the cockpit is the agency's operating system: money (ledger, exposure, enforcement), lifecycle (trials, funnel, churn), client communication (daily stats, dunning), all audited. The M1 event log + ledger is the substrate that makes every later capability a set of queries and jobs rather than a new system. The 10x endpoint (ads that build themselves) is written down in TODOS.md as the v2 flagship, deliberately not scheduled until the control plane earns trust.
